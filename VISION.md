# Vision

Owner: Hung · Confirmed: 2026-10-23 (accepted in PR #5; restructured 2026-11-01)

TalkToBook turns a talk into a reading edition that someone wants to keep.

## Why it exists

Reuse a recording as a course companion, webinar follow-up, newsletter
bonus, or personal book.

## Who it is for

Creators, educators, and readers who own a recording or have rights to it.

## The outcome that matters most

The page is the product; EPUB makes it portable.

## What done looks like

Remove caption timestamps, form readable paragraphs, keep speaker turns
clear, and credit creators at the start. Present a cover, title page, and
readable typography. Three authored demo editions let people inspect the
result before conversion.

## Principles

- Preserve attribution and source rights.
- This repository is the canonical public product.

## Scope

The product includes the CLI, the agent skill, and one local web converter.
Captioned YouTube URLs and local transcript files are inputs. Markdown and
EPUB are core outputs. PDF and Kindle depend on optional installed tools.

## Out of scope

- Private operator workspaces, generated personal books, account credentials,
  and user transcripts in public source.

## Open questions

- No measurable adoption or reading-quality target is recorded. Hung decides.
