# TalkToBook vision

TalkToBook turns a talk into a reading edition that someone wants to keep.
Creators, educators, and readers can reuse a recording they own as a
course companion, webinar follow-up, newsletter bonus, or personal book.

## Intended outcomes

Remove caption timestamps, form readable paragraphs, keep speaker turns
clear, and credit creators at the start. Present a cover, title page, and
readable typography. The page is the product; EPUB makes it portable.
Three authored demo editions let people inspect the result before conversion.

## Scope and boundaries

The product includes the CLI, the agent skill, and one local web converter.
Captioned YouTube URLs and local transcript files are inputs. Markdown and
EPUB are core outputs. PDF and Kindle depend on optional installed tools.

Preserve attribution and source rights. Do not add private operator
workspaces, generated personal books, account credentials, or user
transcripts to public source. This repository is the canonical public
product. Current verification limits belong in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md).
